My Three Work Locations


A&P (1997-2015)

During my time here, I was a Senior Graphic Designer and the right-hand (man or) assistant to the Production Manager and Creative Director on the production side. My main focus was creating and refining weekly print circulars to promote store sales under very tight deadlines. I also stepped up to manage a team of ten artists whenever necessary.

Shoppers (2016-2018)

Following my time at A&P, I had the wonderful opportunity to join the design team for Shoppers Supermarket (Super Valu) in Bowie, Maryland which was a satellite location. I was officially employed by the marketing agency Ivie & Associates—headquartered out of Flower Mound, Texas—which has since been acquired by Quad/Graphics after my stay there.

Enter
Clark Printing (2018-2019)

Clark Printing was a great chapter for me because I got to work alongside some incredibly talented artists on a wide variety of exciting projects. Once again, I was right in my element, designing weekly circulars and store signs on tight schedules. I handled the advertisements for several prominent NYC area supermarket chains, including Metropolitan, Giunta’s, and Pioneer.
